Institutional Preamble

Constitutional & Institutional Mandate

The Indian Institute of Foreign Trade (IIFT), as the official research institution of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, carries a constitutional, moral, and institutional responsibility to ensure equity, credibility, and dignity for all its students across all campuses.

Persistent structural and operational disparities violate public commitments, undermine student confidence, and compromise institutional credibility. This CMP outlines enforceable, time-bound standards.

Core Tenets

Foundational Pillars

  • 1. Campus Parity is Non-Negotiable across all satellite setups.
  • 2. No student should pay a premium fee for institutional disadvantage.
  • 3. Public institutions must remain socially and economically accessible.
  • 4. Official press releases & commitments are legally binding promises.
  • 5. Students are primary stakeholders, not consumers without rights.
Interactive Policy Framework

The Common Minimum Programme

Toggle between critical institutional mandates to inspect problem statements and non-negotiable demands.

CATEGORY A DIRECTIVE: Core institutional failures that directly question the academic credibility, financial ethics, and national stature of the institute.
Mandate A1 Critical Issue

Summer Internship Programme (SIP) Parity

Problem: Satellite campus students excluded from physical, stipend-bearing on-campus drives and forced into off-campus or unpaid roles, impacting long-term CVs.

  • Unified SIP Policy: One common SIP cycle with equal recruiter access across all campuses.
  • Mandatory Stipend: Zero-stipend internships explicitly banned under IIFT branding.
  • Corporate Accountability: Firms unwilling to pay stipends barred from recruiting.
  • Ministry Intervention: Leverage PSU, FIEO, CII, FICCI, ASSOCHAM linkages if corporates fail.
Mandate A2 Critical Issue

Fee Rationalisation & Social Equity

Problem: Fees of ₹19,67,000+ without guaranteed SIP/placement outcomes create severe financial distress for middle/lower-income students in a public institution.

  • Public Cost Breakup: Publicly disclosed cost-breakup & annual audit report.
  • Outcome-Linked Ethics: Fee levels must reflect actual opportunity parity, not branding.
  • Financial Waivers: Mandatory EWS fee waivers & emergency hardship grants.
  • Government Subsidies: Active pursuit of Ministry subsidies for tuition relief.
Mandate A3 Critical Issue

Accountability for Institutional Promises

Problem: Official statements claim identical infrastructure, faculty, and opportunities across campuses, contradicting ground reality.

  • Written Reconciliation: Formal administrative clarification reconciling promises vs reality.
  • Parity Review Committee: Admin, Faculty, and Student body quarterly reporting panel.
  • Compensation Framework: Institutional project compensation if recruiter parity fails.
Mandate A4 Critical Issue

Academic & Infrastructure Readiness

Problem: Delayed course books, functional library opened just 2 days before exams, and non-functional computer labs undermine basic education.

  • Timely Course Books: Mandatory disbursement of books at term start.
  • Functional Library: Operational year-round with qualified librarian present.
  • Working Computer Lab: Fully configured systems and software installed prior to term start.
CATEGORY B DIRECTIVE: Essential academic, operational, transport, and governance standards necessary for day-to-day student welfare and dignity.
Mandate B1

Library Resources Governance

  • Official book-to-student ratio defined.
  • Regular procurement cycles for updated editions.
  • Library hours aligned with academic demands.
  • Permanent, qualified librarian presence.
Mandate B2

Faculty Quality Assurance

  • Mandatory interviews for all guest faculty.
  • Panel must include subject-matter expert faculty.
  • Zero compromise on teaching standards for administrative convenience.
Mandate B3

Budget Governance (IMF/Clubs)

  • Budgeting must NOT rely solely on headcount.
  • Must factor fixed costs & activity viability.
  • Transparent discussion with student body.
  • Avoid treating national institutes like local tuition centers.
Mandate B4

Transport & Mobility

  • Regular shuttle buses between hostel & campus.
  • Fixed, published, and strictly monitored timetables.
  • Transport gaps directly impacting attendance are unacceptable.
Mandate B5

MoU Transparency & Monitoring

  • Timely written status updates on all MoUs (Hostels, Sports facilities, Collaborations).
  • Designated administrative authority held accountable for monitoring agreements.
  • Students must not be admitted without operational MoUs in place.
