Institutional Framework • IMF Policy Representation

Common Minimum Programme (CMP) Portal

A non-negotiable policy framework for campus parity, academic readiness, fee rationalisation, and student dignity. Click below to enter deeper levels of the mandate.

⚠️ EXTREMELY NON-NEGOTIABLE DIRECTIVES: Core institutional failures directly impacting academic equity, student career security, and legal promises.
Mandate A1 Critical

Summer Internship Programme (SIP) Parity

Problem: Satellite campus students excluded from physical, stipend-bearing on-campus drives and forced into off-campus or unpaid roles.

• Unified SIP Policy: One common SIP cycle with equal recruiter access across all campuses.

• Mandatory Stipend Norm: Zero-stipend internships explicitly banned under IIFT branding.

• Corporate Accountability: Firms unwilling to pay stipends barred from recruiting.

• Ministry Intervention: Leverage PSU, FIEO, CII, FICCI, ASSOCHAM linkages if corporates fail.

Mandate A2 Critical

Fee Rationalisation & Social Accessibility

Problem: Fees of ₹19,67,000+ without guaranteed placement outcomes create severe financial distress for middle/lower-income students.

• Public Cost Breakup: Publicly disclosed cost-breakup & annual audit report.

• Outcome-Linked Fees: Fee levels must reflect actual opportunity parity, not branding.

• Financial Waivers: Mandatory EWS fee waivers & emergency hardship grants.

• Ministry Subsidies: Active pursuit of Ministry subsidies for tuition relief.

Mandate A3 Critical

Accountability for Institutional Promises

Problem: Official statements claim identical infrastructure and opportunities across campuses, contradicting ground reality.

• Written Reconciliation: Formal administrative clarification reconciling promises vs reality.

• Parity Review Committee: Admin, Faculty, and Student body quarterly reporting panel.

• Corrective Safeguards: Institutional project compensation if recruiter parity fails.

Mandate A4 Critical

Academic & Infrastructure Readiness

Problem: Delayed course books, functional library opened just 2 days before exams, and non-functional computer labs.

• Timely Course Books: Mandatory disbursement of books at term start.

• Functional Library: Operational year-round with qualified librarian present.

• Computer Lab Readiness: Fully configured systems and software installed prior to term start.

🛠️ OPERATIONAL & GOVERNANCE MANDATES: Academic quality assurance, transport mobility, club budgeting, and MoU transparency.
Mandate B1

Library Resources

  • Official book-to-student ratio defined.
  • Regular procurement cycles for updated editions.
  • Permanent, qualified librarian presence.
Mandate B2

Faculty Quality Assurance

  • Mandatory interviews for guest faculty.
  • Panel must include subject-matter experts.
  • Zero compromise on teaching standards.
Mandate B3

Budget Governance

  • Budgeting must NOT rely solely on headcount.
  • Must factor fixed operational costs.
  • Transparent discussions with student body.
Mandate B4

Transport & Mobility

  • Regular shuttle buses between hostel & campus.
  • Fixed, published, and monitored timetables.
  • Transport gaps impacting attendance are unacceptable.
Mandate B5

MoU Transparency & Monitoring

  • Timely written status updates on all MoUs (Hostels, Sports, Collaborations).
  • Designated administrative authority held accountable for monitoring agreements.
  • Students must not be admitted without operational MoUs in place.
Institutional Preamble

International Management Forum (IMF) Mandate

The Indian Institute of Foreign Trade (IIFT), functioning as the official research institution of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, is obligated to maintain uniform academic standards, infrastructure, student welfare, and opportunity parity across all campuses.

Core Non-Negotiable Principles:

  1. Campus Parity is Non-Negotiable.
  2. No Student Should Pay a Premium for Disadvantage.
  3. Public Institutions Must Remain Socially Accessible.
  4. Promises Made by Institutions Are Binding Commitments.
  5. Students Are Stakeholders, Not Consumers Without Rights.
